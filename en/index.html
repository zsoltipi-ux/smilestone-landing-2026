How it works

Three steps that already happen in your lab.

We didn't reinvent the wheel. We made the process you already run transparent — together with labs, for nearly ten years.

1

The order comes in

From anywhere: the dentist submits it on their own portal, you enter it, or you upload the PDF from the scanner e-mail and the system reads the data.

  • Tooth chart, shade, due date, notes
  • STL, PLY, exocad designs, photos on the ticket

Assign production phases to products, delegate them to colleagues. Every status change is time-stamped — from a phone or a QR code too.

Frequently asked

What we always get asked in demos.

If yours isn't here, write to us — or call; most lab managers decide over the phone.

How long until we use it for real?

We do the setup (price list, production phases, ordering dentists) together with you. Send your price list in any format and photos of the last two weeks' work tickets, and within days you get a ready system with your own cases in it. The proven path: one dentist 100% for a month, then a joint month-end statement.

What happens to the paper tickets?

They can stay as long as you need. Many labs run in parallel for a month or two: they photograph the paper and attach it to the digital ticket. The point is that data is entered once — everything is calculated from there.

Do dentists have to pay?

No. Dentist users are free and unlimited. The lab pays, based on the lab's volume.

What if my dentists don't want to use it?

Then you enter their work — it works the same way. Experience says: start with two or three dentists you get on well with; the others follow because of the status visibility.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on PC, laptop, tablet and phone. The QR code on the ticket opens the case straight on your phone.

What exactly does it cost?

€ 70 + VAT monthly base fee with the first 75 cases included; € 1 per case between 76 and 200, € 0.66 per case above 200. Unlimited users, full feature set. Use the slider to work out yours.

Who sees prices and technician pay?

Whatever you set. There are manager, lab lead and technician roles, overridable per person: for example the lab lead sees every ticket but no prices; the technician only the work assigned to them.

Is my data safe?

SSL/HTTPS, regular backups, GDPR compliance. Login can be restricted to a fixed IP address on request. After delivery a ticket can't be deleted, only archived — every change is traceable with a time stamp.

Does it help with MDR compliance?

Yes. Traceability of tickets, materials and phases provides the documentation — without extra hours. It also meets what German clients expect.

Is there training and support?

Yes. We run lab-tailored training for the team and for the ordering dentists, and we review the first month-end statement together. After that you reach us by phone and e-mail.
